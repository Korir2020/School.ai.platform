## 1. Product objective and rules

Build a genuinely usable, independent Personalized Learning ecosystem inside Marian. It is a separate learning domain and product—not an extension of exams, marks, report analytics, early-warning/weakness profiling, billing, portfolios, or school administration workflows.

The ecosystem must ultimately serve school-linked and independent public learners, as well as teachers and administrators who also want to learn. A person’s job title must not limit what they may explore. School-linked and public identities and data must remain strictly separate. School administrators, deputies/limited staff, teachers, guardians, and platform super-administrators must not be given interchangeable privileges.

Learning should be self-directed, interest-led, multilingual, age-appropriate, and user-controlled. English and Kiswahili are priorities. Curriculum support must be configurable for Kenyan CBC Grades 1–12, Cambridge, international frameworks, and non-curriculum learning; do not hardcode one curriculum. Beyond-curriculum discovery should eventually include AI, computing/programming, robotics/technology, science/astronomy, business/entrepreneurship, financial literacy, languages/communication, leadership, creativity/design/arts, research/critical thinking, history/geography, agriculture/environment, health/general wellbeing education, professional development, life skills/personal growth, and general/global knowledge.

Preserve existing Marian functions and data: examinations and calculations, marks, publication and archived reports, student management, billing/fees, migrations, and existing navigation/authentication behavior. Do not use exam marks, weaknesses, risk scores, report text, or negative labels for learning recommendations. Add learning functionality through its own domain and additive integrations. Certificates are not part of the initial product. Offline learning is future work and must not be claimed until implemented and tested.

AI must be provider-independent, server-side, age-appropriate, privacy-reviewed, safety-controlled, rate/cost-limited, and disabled unless a real approved provider and credentials are configured. Never expose API keys in the frontend or fake AI responses. Generated publishable content requires appropriate human review.

## 3. Canonical activity sequence

### Activity 1 — Product vision, architecture audit, and implementation blueprint

### Activity 2 — Learning-domain data foundation
**Status: DONE.** Added the dedicated Django learning app, additive initial migration, learning-owned profiles/account-scope records, invitations/guardian-approval state, frameworks/topics, resources/translations, paths/items, preferences/goals/saved-item records, activity events, projects/work, recommendation and AI-usage metadata, and audit records. Added constraints, indexes, and tests. No existing student rows were linked automatically and no academic or financial data was repurposed.

### Activity 3 — School-linked learner identity and permission foundation
**Status: PARTIAL.** Implemented full-school-admin invitation operations, one-time hashed claim codes, linked-guardian approval for minors/unknown age, student account claim, learning identity resolution, school-scope checks, and suspension enforcement. School learners can use Marian authentication. Remaining account work is tracked in Activity 11: verified public registration, real delivery/recovery flows, the guardian experience, and approved policies for public minors and unknown age. Existing email/SMS delivery must not be represented as active when it is not configured.

### Activity 4 — Learner profile and preferences API
**Status: DONE.** Implemented own-profile `GET/PATCH` with allowlisted fields, locale and preference validation, active learner/consent checks, and ownership isolation. No client-supplied school, student, or user identity establishes authority.

### Activity 5 — Learner-owned goals API
**Status: DONE.** Implemented own-goal list/create/update/soft-close endpoints, bounded input, topic/path visibility validation, and ownership protection. Goals do not read exam, report, or financial data.

### Activity 6 — Read-only learning catalogue API
**Status: DONE.** Implemented authenticated topic, resource, and path browse/detail endpoints. Scope, publication/reviewer metadata, translation approval, language, age suitability, licensing/HTTPS, school suspension, and path-item visibility are checked. Private storage keys are not returned. File-only resources remain hidden unless Activity 10 provides a clean attachment and the parent resource passes all current catalogue gates. This activity did not create publishing workflows or screens.

### Activity 7 — Learner saved-items API
**Status: DONE.** Added learner-owned save/list/remove behavior for currently visible resources and paths. The authenticated account is resolved to its own eligible learning profile; current publication, review, language, age, licensing, and tenant visibility are rechecked on save and list. Repeated saves and collection removals are idempotent; saved-record IDs belonging to another learner return 404. Lists are capped at 200, and stale or newly hidden items are omitted. No migration was required.

### Activity 8 — Path participation and validated progress events
**Status: PARTIAL / SAFE CORE IMPLEMENTED.** Added idempotent start/follow for the current learner-visible published path version, owner-only participation and event-history APIs, 8 KiB event-body limit, required UUID idempotency keys, strict `started`/`progress`/`feedback` schemas, and server-side current visibility checks for resources/path items. Activity 17 now adds a server-created completion event only after a current-version quiz is scored; this evidence is owner-visible and contains no answers or score. It does not complete path participation or a path step. Client time, arbitrary fields, completion claims, submissions, and totals remain rejected; duration is never recorded and no click/time reward exists. No teacher/admin raw history route was added.

### Activity 9 — Learning content governance and publishing services
**Status: PARTIAL / PUBLICATION POLICY-GATED.** Added school-scoped resource/path drafts, submission, independent full-school-admin review and approval, request-changes, archive/retire, tenant review queue, immutable content snapshots/version history, and minimal audit events. Authorization derives from real Marian teacher and full-school-admin profiles; deputies, platform staff, and superusers receive no implied authority, and authors cannot review/approve their own content. Shared-catalogue authoring is not exposed. Submissions validate source/provenance, licence or usage-rights notes, attribution, language, age range, HTTPS for external links, and reviewed human translation workflows for resource and path metadata. External-origin resource creation is link-only; no external fetch or scraping occurs. `publish` always returns a policy-gate 503 and never changes content to `published` until licensing/rights, moderation/review, and shared-catalogue ownership decisions are separately approved. Path translations are reviewed/stored but are not yet localized by the learner catalogue; nothing new can be published through these endpoints.

### Activity 10 — Secure private-file lifecycle
**Status: IMPLEMENTED / DEPLOYMENT-GATED.** Added a private storage abstraction, school-authorized Studio upload/list/delete/reviewer-download APIs, and learner download streaming that rechecks current publication, review, age, language, consent, suspension, and tenant visibility on every request. Development-only filesystem storage is outside `MEDIA_ROOT`, rejects path overlap/symlink roots, uses restrictive directory/file permissions, and is ignored by source control. Production storage returns 503 until a durable private backend is explicitly configured; that backend must implement `save/open/delete` and declare `is_private=True` and `is_durable=True`. Uploads accept only normalized PDF, JPEG, and PNG bytes; uploads are capped at 10 MiB each, 10 active attachments and 50 MiB per resource, with a 100-page PDF limit and 10,000-dimension/40-megapixel image caps. PDFs are rebuilt without JavaScript/actions/attachments; images are decoded and re-encoded; archives, SVG, animation, malformed files, and encrypted PDFs are rejected. Every upload is recorded as quarantined before scanning. A configured ClamAV-compatible scanner must report clean before a file can be downloaded, submitted, or reviewed; missing, infected, timed-out, or errored scans remain inaccessible. Downloads are authenticated streams, never public URLs or bearer links, use attachment/octet-stream, no-store and `nosniff` headers, and do not expose original filenames or storage keys. Deletion revokes access first, removes bytes immediately, retains a minimal tombstone, and can be retried after storage failure. No automatic retention interval is invented. **Product-owner decisions still required before production use:** select/approve the durable private-storage provider/backend, scanner service and operating configuration, and the retention/audit policy. Until those are configured, production upload is unavailable and unscanned assets remain quarantined; Activity 9 publishing remains independently policy-gated.

### Activity 11 — Complete account lifecycle and guardian operations
**Status: PARTIAL / DEPENDENCY-GATED; safe core implemented.** The approved age policy is confirmed and implemented: under 10 is school-managed only; ages 10–17 require verified guardian involvement and consent for independent access; ages 18+ may self-register after real age/contact verification; unknown age is denied protected learning features. The configurable independent minimum defaults to 10 and cannot be lowered below 10. Reuses Marian authentication and roster-derived age for school accounts. Added provider capability reporting, always-disabled independent registration, guardian linked-learner summaries/consent withdrawal, bounded/audited school-admin summaries and account list/state controls, assigned-teacher minimal progress summaries, non-destructive lifecycle request/cancel, conservative age migration, invitation delivery status, safe reissue after expiry, and responsive public/guardian/learner/admin screens. Existing Marian login and generic password recovery are unchanged. Real provider registration/verification/delivery/recovery, new guardian verification/consent grants, automated retention/deletion, and assigned-case moderator workflows remain unimplemented and disabled; the provider interface/path alone cannot activate them. See `docs/activity11-account-operations.md` for routes, configuration, tests and release gates.

### Activity 12 — Learner learning experience
**Status: IMPLEMENTED / CATALOGUE-CONTENT-POLICY-GATED.** Added a distinct responsive/mobile-first Marian learner shell for `student` and `public_learner` roles, using shared UI controls and existing authenticated learning APIs. It includes discovery/search, resource detail/download, learning-path list/detail/start, private goals (create/edit/state/soft-close), saved-item list/remove, continue/recent activity, and allowlisted self-reported progress check-ins. Loading, empty, error, and account-unavailable states are explicit; language selection persists the learner’s content-language preference and respects server-supported locales. The existing Marian login is reused, public learners receive no school-only navigation, and non-learner role tabs/hash routes are unchanged. No new backend endpoint, model, or migration was added; the existing goal-link validation/serialization and resource/path list cap were hardened to reuse current catalogue visibility and correctly count visible rows. The home privacy text distinguishes non-public activity from limited progress summaries visible to assigned teachers for school-managed learners, and footer text now exceeds the WCAG AA contrast threshold. Existing server-side age, consent, account, school-suspension, content visibility and ownership gates remain authoritative. Activity 9 publication is still hard-gated, so catalogue results depend on already-published eligible content; path-step instructions are not yet localized by the learner catalogue. No client completion, time-tracking/rewards, AI tutor, or premium checkout claim is made.

### Activity 13 — Teacher Studio
**Status: IMPLEMENTED / PUBLISHING-POLICY-GATED.** Added a role-specific, lazy-loaded Teacher Learning Studio. Teachers can browse/search currently published free resources and programmes from shared and their own school catalogue scopes, inspect their own drafts and manage only their own school resource/path drafts, submit them for separate review, and recommend currently visible free reviewed/published resources or programmes to active, consent/age-eligible students in assigned streams. Every recommendation rechecks the student assignment and current catalogue eligibility; learner accept/dismiss actions are owner-only and audited. Teacher progress is limited to learning participation summaries for assigned learners; DOB, age band, account status, marks, attendance, fees, report cards, learner prompts, and work are not returned. A school-controlled Teacher Studio switch also closes the legacy individual progress-summary endpoint. No automatic scoring or Activity 15 recommendation algorithm is introduced. New content remains private until review; the Activity 9 hard publication gate still blocks publishing.

### Activity 14 — Learning Administration Centre
**Status: IMPLEMENTED / SCHOOL-SCOPED.** Added an isolated, full-school-admin Learning Centre for school-scoped aggregate usage, resource/programme and translation review queues, approved material/programme status, and school settings. New additive migrations `learning.0008_learningschoolsettings` and `learning.0009_learningschoolsettings_learn_school_age_min_plausible` add per-school learner-access and Teacher Studio/recommendation switches plus a bounded 0–120 age range; settings reads/updates and review-queue reads are audited. Only active full administrators may change these settings; deputies, superusers, staff, teachers, and conflicting dual-role accounts are denied. School age bounds are enforced on protected learner APIs and can only narrow access; existing age, guardian-consent, account, resource and publication checks remain in force. The centre shows aggregate school use, not a roster or independent-public learner records. Translation approval does not publish parent content. Activity 9 publication remains deliberately hard-gated.

### Activity 15 — Explainable deterministic recommendations
**Status: IMPLEMENTED / CONTENT-AND-ENTITLEMENT-GATED.** Added `POST /api/learning/v1/recommendations/generate/` as a separate deterministic rules engine; it uses only the caller's own selected topics, active learner goals, preferred language/format/difficulty, and recent visible learning-event target identities. Event text/details, duration, marks, exams, reports, attendance, fees, risk data, prompts, and demographic profiling are not inputs. Candidate resources/programmes are rechecked for publication/review, language/translation, age, tenant, suspension, and current subscription entitlement; the engine is bounded to 160 resources, 80 paths, 80 recent events, and eight active suggestions per learner. It stores an algorithm version, allowlisted source-signal codes, bilingual reason text, 30-day expiry, learner dismissal state, and database protection against duplicate active auto-suggestions. Existing teacher-selected recommendations remain separate and remain visible when automatic personalization is paused. Added a bilingual responsive preferences panel for topic interests (up to 10), format, difficulty, and opt-out. User-dismissed targets do not reappear via the automated path. The engine is deterministic, not AI. Catalogue publication remains policy-gated, so no result is guaranteed if no eligible reviewed content exists; payment processing and premium activation remain non-live.

## 4. Product-owner decisions and dependencies

The user has confirmed these product decisions: (1) active Marian students receive Personalized Learning through their existing accounts without an unnecessary second invitation; (2) independent learners may register only under the approved age/guardian rules; (3) every eligible learner must have meaningful free learning access; (4) premium is an individual learner subscription, separate from school subscriptions and Marian school-fee accounts, and an eligible minor's parent/guardian may be the payer; (5) pricing stays configurable until separately approved; and (6) unverified payment responses must never activate premium. Implementation status is not the same as approval: Activity 11 still needs to replace/complete its invitation-dependent school-linking path so existing eligible roster accounts are provisioned without a new invite; free access depends on separately approved and published licensed content; guardian-as-payer and pricing workflows are not implemented; and no payment provider is active. The approved age threshold/basic guardian policy remains confirmed: under 10 is school-managed only, ages 10–17 require verified guardian involvement/consent for independent access, ages 18+ may self-register after real age/contact checks, and unknown age is denied protected learning features. Do not claim provider-dependent registration, contact/age/guardian verification, message delivery, recovery, automated retention/deletion, assigned-case moderator access, live AI, live payments, or catalogue publication until real integrations, credentials, operating policies, and test evidence exist. Remaining gates include licensing/rights allowlists, moderation and shared-catalogue ownership, durable storage/scanning/retention, AI provider/contracts/data-retention/usage budgets, analytics retention, moderator policy, and production rollout requirements.
